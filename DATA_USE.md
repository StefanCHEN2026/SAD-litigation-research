## 1. Permitted uses

You may use the data for:

- non-commercial academic research;
- teaching and classroom demonstration;
- replication and verification of the accompanying paper;
- non-commercial policy analysis, with attribution.

You must cite the paper and this repository. See `CITATION.cff`.

## 2. Prohibited uses

You may **not**:

- use the data for commercial purposes, including paid consulting, litigation support, or product development, without prior written permission;
- attempt to re-identify any individual, seller, store, or entity;
- use the data to contact, target, harass, blacklist, or take legal or commercial action against any seller, platform, or rights holder;
- redistribute the data, in whole or in part, except as embedded in a non-commercial academic publication with attribution;
- combine the data with other datasets to identify specific sellers or individuals;
- present the data as a finding that any particular seller or company infringed any right.

## 3. Nature of the data

- The underlying court records contain **allegations** by plaintiffs. A defendant’s appearance in a docket is not a finding of liability.
- The aggregated statistics are research estimates. They are not a census, not a legal opinion, and not a compliance determination.
- The synthetic data are generated by the simulation model. They do not describe real sellers.
- Raw case-level data from sellerdefense, 知产宝, and 知产查 are **not** included. They remain subject to the terms of their sources.
